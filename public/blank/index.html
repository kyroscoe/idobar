Additional Bar Options

Bar add-on options

You bring the alcohol ; we can bring everything else to help from having to bring in any more items than necessary.

  • Soda cans
  • Iced tea & lemonade
  • Coffee & accompaniments
  • Plastic cups, napkins & stir sticks
  • Bar mixers
  • Garnishes
  • Bar-drink ice

Mix and match options or ask about a complete beverage package. Complete beverage packages include ice for bar drinks only. Final selections are due at least two weeks before the event; package quantities are based on your total guest count.

Ask for a current quote and confirm the supplies, staffing, and quantities for your event. Alcohol is not included.
