Common Questions

Have something else in mind?
We’d love to hear it.

Do you provide the alcohol?

You provide the alcohol. We offer professional bartending and optional bar add-ons, including beverage service supplies. We’ll discuss what you need for your event when you inquire.

What kinds of events do you serve?

We serve weddings, private parties, corporate events, and special celebrations throughout Central Ohio.

Can we book bartenders without an add-on package?

Yes. We offer professional bartending staff as well as customizable bar add-on options. Tell us about your venue and what is already provided.

What should we include in an inquiry?

Your event date, venue or location, estimated guest count, and the kind of bar service you have in mind are a great place to start.

Can you help with wedding coordination, too?

Our sister brand, I Do Coordinations, offers wedding and event coordination. Visit the sister site to explore its services.
